PROCURA — AI PROCUREMENT CALLER AGENT
Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Product: Procura
Module: AI Voice Procurement & Negotiation Agent
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Enable Procura to autonomously contact only buyer-selected suppliers, conduct natural voice conversations, understand the supplier's language, negotiate within buyer-defined constraints, extract commercial offers, and return structured results to the buyer.

1. Product Objective

Procura's AI Caller Agent is responsible for contacting suppliers selected by the buyer and negotiating procurement terms on the buyer's behalf.

The agent should behave like a professional procurement representative, rather than a robotic IVR.

The system should:

Call only suppliers explicitly selected by the buyer.
Use the buyer's name during the conversation.
Start the conversation in English or Hindi.
Detect the supplier's spoken language automatically.
Understand English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and mixed-language speech where supported by the selected voice stack.
Respond naturally in the supplier's language.
Handle pauses, interruptions, hesitations and conversational turn-taking.
Negotiate price and commercial terms.
Never exceed the buyer's maximum authorized price.
Never reveal the buyer's maximum budget.
Never place or confirm a purchase.
Extract structured offers from conversations.
Track negotiation rounds.
Store call transcripts and negotiation history.
Present final offers to the buyer for approval.
2. Core Product Principle

Procura discovers suppliers. The buyer chooses who gets contacted. The AI negotiates only with those explicitly selected suppliers.

The AI must not independently decide to call suppliers.

23. Maximum Price Rule

This is a hard constraint.

Example:

Target = ₹6,500
Maximum = ₹8,000

Supplier:

"₹8,500 is the lowest we can do."

Agent:

₹8,500 > ₹8,000

"I wouldn't be able to proceed at that price. Is there any possibility of bringing it below ₹8,000?"

₹8,001+

30. Total Cost Calculation

The agent should not compare unit price alone.

Calculate:

Total Cost =
Product Cost
+ Shipping
+ Installation
+ Applicable Charges
+ Other Negotiated Costs

For example:

₹7,100 × 500
= ₹35,50,000

Shipping
= ₹20,000

Total
= ₹35,70,000

The dashboard should expose assumptions such as GST inclusion/exclusion.

31. Call Script

The agent should not follow a rigid script.

Instead it should use conversational objectives.

Opening

"Hi, this is Sadwik. I'm looking to source 500 ergonomic office chairs for our office in Chennai. Is this something you can supply?"

Supplier confirms

"Great. What would be your best bulk price for the full quantity?"

Supplier quotes

"Okay, ₹7,600. And is that inclusive of GST and delivery?"

Negotiation

"Since we're looking at 500 units, is there some room to improve that price?"

Closing

"Okay, I'll record that as your best offer and we'll review it."

   follow the configured disclosure policy honestly.

6. Never reveal the buyer's maximum budget.

7. Never exceed the buyer's authorized maximum price.

8. Never invent supplier information.

9. Never invent buyer requirements.

10. Never invent an offer.

14. Respect maximum negotiation rounds.

52.12 Negotiation Rules

The following rules are mandatory:

ONLY selected suppliers can be called.

NEVER call an unselected supplier.

NEVER exceed the buyer's maximum price.

NEVER reveal the buyer's maximum price.

NEVER reveal internal negotiation strategy.

NEVER fabricate an offer.

NEVER fabricate supplier information.

NEVER fabricate buyer requirements.

NEVER claim an order has been placed.

NEVER make a binding purchase commitment.

NEVER accept a contract on behalf of the buyer.

NEVER negotiate beyond the configured maximum rounds.

ALWAYS record meaningful supplier offers.

ALWAYS preserve negotiation context across language changes.

ALWAYS ask for clarification when an offer is ambiguous.

ALWAYS return the final result to the buyer.

Procura's AI Caller is a buyer-authorized, multilingual, real-time voice procurement agent that contacts only explicitly selected suppliers, negotiates within strict buyer-defined constraints, extracts commercial offers, and returns them to the buyer for final approval
